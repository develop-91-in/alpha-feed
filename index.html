How this edition works

  • Finance only: Sports, Bollywood, entertainment, celebrity and general politics are filtered out. Global News covers geopolitics only where it has financial consequences.
  • Hourly refresh: In-browser refresh runs every hour while the page is open. True background refresh when the page is closed requires the included GitHub Actions workflow + Vercel deploy — see README. Muse alone does not run background jobs.
  • 24-hour retention: Articles are kept only if their original publication time is within the last 24 hours. Older articles are removed on every refresh and replaced by newly available relevant news.
  • Market data: Ticker shows DATA NOT CONNECTED until a verified feed returns data. Sample prices are never shown as live. MCX Gold / Silver / Crude are derived from COMEX × USD/INR and labelled as such — not official MCX.
  • Verification: Every story keeps its original source, publication time (IST) and link. Summaries are short excerpts, not full copyrighted text.
